EducationRev Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

EducationRev provides education, planning, study, wellbeing, and AI-supported learning tools for students, teachers, and school leaders.

Information We Collect

We may collect account information such as name, email address, sign-in provider, profile settings, portal selections, and education workspace content that users choose to create or save in the app.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide sign-in, account access, personalised learning tools, progress tracking, saved study content, support, security, and service improvement.

Google Sign-In

If a user signs in with Google, EducationRev uses Google account authentication to identify the user and connect them to their account. We do not sell Google user data.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may use trusted service providers such as Firebase and Google Cloud to host, authenticate, store, and secure the application.
